Our Story

Founded by Abi Huntsman, our company was born out of a passion for bringing wedding visions to life. We believe every couple deserves a magical and stress-free planning experience. Our goal is to help two people come together and create new memories, finding out what they love together as one. We hope our company can make people happy and bring others to enjoy the same experience as the bride and groom.

Why choose Dream Wedding Events?

We offer personalized, customizable wedding plans designed to make your special day a dream come true. Our team works closely with you to ensure every detail is exactly as you've always imagined.
